### 🌸 about me

I'm Nnenna (most people call me Melani), a Computer Science graduate from Port Harcourt, Nigeria. I turn messy data into clear decisions, help train AI models by judging their work, and build web tools when a project needs one. My sights are on **AI in biomedicine**, computational biology and, one day, pharmacogenomics.

- 🤖 currently: evaluating a voice AI agent's responses (RLHF) with **Meridial**
- 💼 founder of **Capstone Lab**, a data and technology consultancy
- 👩🏾‍💻 leading an 8-person web team at **Averixa Digital**
- 🩺 learning: data engineering (Docker, dbt, pipelines) and health data standards like FHIR
- 🤝 open to: remote data, AI training and web roles, plus health data internships and collaborations
